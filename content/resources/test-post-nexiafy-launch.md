Nexiafy today announced the general availability of its unified AI platform, bringing together automation, predictive analytics, and custom assistants in a single workspace for product and engineering teams.

**Built for teams that ship**

The platform is designed around a simple idea: most teams do not need another disconnected AI tool. They need a coherent layer that connects to their data, respects their policies, and fits into existing workflows.

Our launch focus includes:

- Workflow automation with human-in-the-loop checkpoints
- Predictive dashboards tied to your product metrics
- Custom assistants trained on your documentation and APIs

> “We built Nexiafy so teams can experiment quickly without giving up control over data and compliance,” said the product team.
