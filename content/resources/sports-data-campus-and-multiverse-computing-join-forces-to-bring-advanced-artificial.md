Sports Data Campus and Multiverse Computing have established a strategic alliance to bring advanced artificial intelligence to the global football and sports ecosystem.

**An alliance for data-driven football**

Professional football is entering an era in which data, artificial intelligence, and optimisation play a growing role in performance, scouting, talent development, and fan engagement.

**Shared vision**

The agreement establishes a framework to explore joint initiatives in education, applied projects, technology, and business development—making advanced capabilities accessible to clubs, leagues, and federations while preparing professionals to apply these tools in real-world settings.
