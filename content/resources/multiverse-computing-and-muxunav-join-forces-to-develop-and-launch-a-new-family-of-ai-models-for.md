Multiverse Computing and Muxunav have announced a strategic partnership to co-develop a new family of AI models tailored to regulated financial services and unattended commerce.

**Focus areas**

The collaboration targets identity verification (KYC), fraud prevention, and voice assistance, with an initial rollout on Muxunav's unattended payment devices.

**Why it matters**

Regulated industries need efficient models that can run with strong governance. This partnership aims to deliver specialised capabilities without compromising compliance requirements.
